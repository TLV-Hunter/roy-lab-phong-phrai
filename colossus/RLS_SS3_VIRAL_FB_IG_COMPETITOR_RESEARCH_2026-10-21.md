## MARKET INSIGHTS — HYPOTHESES, NOT PROVEN USA DEMAND STATISTICS
A. Extreme unexpected animal behaviour (Bighorn Ram breakthrough) may outperform pure fantasy size. Clear real-animal body, anatomical motion, grounded fixed object/people for size, unusual reaction and environmental consequence. Single dramatic scene is enough; no 50KM scene required.
B. Battle/versus works for explicit immediate stakes: predator vs predator or near miss, surprising reversal. Instagram T-Rex vs Spinosaurus indexed 5.9M views and 180K likes (different sources). Not all real wildlife fights perform well or represent animal welfare.
C. Cryptid/camera trap lends the SS1 archive aesthetic to a real visible creature; unlike SS1's minimal silhouette, SS3 could reveal living bodies in one grounded shot, as long as no physics/anatomy failure.
D. Keep English-first international accessibility, original creator-led story, factual AI disclosure, natural Foley. No fake documentary breaking news nor third party copyrighted footage.
E. The raw follower target 10K cannot be guaranteed without starting baseline, post cadence, insights and actual confirmed Meta publishing connection. Follow conversion should be measured after each 24h/72h.
